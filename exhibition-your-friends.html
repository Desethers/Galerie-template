Your friends

A presentation of recent paintings and works on paper exploring friendship, memory, and shared light. The exhibition brings together a focused selection of pieces conceived as a single environment.

Arranged as a sequence of rooms, the works invite a slow reading: color fields, soft gradients, and restrained surfaces echo the quiet of the gallery itself. The title Your friends signals intimacy without anecdote—an open field for the viewer to inhabit.

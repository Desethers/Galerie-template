Sun Dog — Your friends

A presentation of recent paintings and works on paper exploring friendship, memory, and shared light. The exhibition brings together a focused selection of pieces conceived as a single environment. New and recent pieces are brought together here for the first time as one installation.

Arranged as a sequence of rooms, the works invite a slow reading: color fields, soft gradients, and restrained surfaces echo the quiet of the gallery itself. The title Your friends signals intimacy without anecdote—an open field for the viewer to inhabit.

Private viewing and availability: contact the gallery.

For press images, checklist, and media inquiries, please contact the gallery.

Exhibition text

Presented as one continuous sequence, the works below extend the show’s themes—friendship, memory, and light—into individual canvases and works on paper, each conceived for this installation.

read full exhibition text

The hanging follows the rhythm of the gallery: broader fields alternate with tighter formats, so the visit unfolds like a walk through connected rooms rather than a checklist of objects. Color is held deliberately low, letting surface and edge carry the emotional register of the title Your friends.

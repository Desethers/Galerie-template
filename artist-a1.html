Artists / Sun Dog

Sun Dog

Born 1960, Oklahoma, USA — Lives and works in New York

Sun Dog explores the boundaries of landscape and abstraction through a deeply personal visual vocabulary. His paintings, often rendered in rich, saturated color fields, evoke a contemplative stillness that hovers between representation and pure sensation.

Working primarily with oil on canvas, his practice distills nature into its most essential forms — solitary trees, expansive skies, and luminous horizons emerge from layers of pigment with an almost meditative quality. The work invites a prolonged encounter, rewarding patience with subtle shifts in tone and light.

Born and raised in Oklahoma, he absorbed the vastness of the plains and the drama of changing weather long before he committed those sensations to canvas. After moving to New York in the late 1980s, he found in the city’s museums and its restless pace a counterpoint that sharpened his appetite for silence, distance, and slow looking.

His process is deliberately unhurried: thin veils of paint are laid down, scraped back, and rebuilt until the surface holds a kind of weather of its own. Color is never merely descriptive; it carries temperature, memory, and mood. Figures rarely appear, yet the paintings often feel inhabited — as if someone had just stepped out of the frame, leaving warmth in the air.

Over the past three decades his work has been shown in solo and group exhibitions across North America and Europe. Pieces reside in public and private collections that value contemporary painting unafraid of beauty, duration, and emotional breadth. He continues to work from a light-filled studio in Brooklyn, where west-facing windows throw long shadows across the floor and remind him, daily, of the horizon he never quite left behind.

Visitors often describe the experience of standing before his large canvases as similar to watching the sky change at dusk: nothing seems to move, and yet everything does. That slowness is not a trick but a discipline — a refusal to shout when a whisper will carry further across time.
